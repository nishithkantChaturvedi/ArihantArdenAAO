AAO Board – 2025

We are pleased to introduce the newly elected board of the Arihant Arden Apartment Owners Association for the year 2025. This team is committed to improving the quality of life for all residents by ensuring transparency, safety, and community development.

The board is led by Nishith Kant Chaturvedi as President, supported by:

  • R K Posh – Vice President
  • Lokesh Tyagi – Secretary
  • Ashok K Singh – Treasurer
  • Ravi Shanker – Joint Secretary
  • Priyanka Jain – Joint Treasurer

Our Executive Members include: N K Singh, Satyam Srivastava, Sanjay Chaudhary, and Varun Girdhar.

We look forward to working together with the entire community to make Arihant Arden a model of cooperative living.
