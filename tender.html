Notice Inviting Proposals for Independent External Audit

Date: 20 June 2026

The Arihant Arden Apartment Owners Association invites proposals from experienced ICAI-registered Chartered Accountant firms for conducting an Independent External Audit of the Society for FY 2025-26.

The audit will cover financial transactions, statutory compliances, contracts, procurement, capital expenditure, internal controls, governance processes and risk assessment.

Eligibility

  • ICAI registered CA Firm with minimum 10 years of practice.
  • Experience of auditing at least 3 residential societies having 750+ flats during the last 5 years.
  • Engagement Partner should be an FCA with minimum 15 years of experience.
  • The firm should not be the current statutory auditor of the Society.

The RFP document is available at RFP for Audit (PDF).

Email: admin@arihantardenaoa.org, president@arihantardenaoa.org
